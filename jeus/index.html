Biomedical AI agent · Manuscript under review

Ask your data a question.

Jeus is an AI agent that answers research questions from biomedical data. Give it a folder of data and a question; it analyses the data with an open-weight or proprietary model, records every step, and returns an answer. This page shows the worked examples from our manuscript and a few of its headline results.

Peak resident memory of the agent process, as a multiple of Jeus's, over 28 benchmark summaries of Codex CLI, Claude Code and Biomni A1. Model servers are excluded for every agent; Biomni A1 runs its analyses inside its own process.

24–81%Lower model-access costs

With Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-6 Sol, Jeus's recorded model-access charges were lower than the other agent's in 14 of 16 benchmark comparisons, by 24 to 81 percent. Judging is excluded.

The manuscript also evaluates 24 clinical prediction tasks, 12 of classification and 12 of survival, with the same agents and models, and gives confidence intervals, the proprietary-model comparisons and the complete resource accounting for everything shown here.

Manuscript

Submitted for publication.

The manuscript compares Jeus with Codex CLI and Claude Code on five biomedical benchmarks and 24 clinical prediction tasks, and with Biomni A1 on BixBench, Verified-50 and eight classification tasks, using the same open-weight and proprietary models for every agent. It reports task performance, tokens, model-access costs and the memory of the agent process. The preprint, the code and the data release will be linked here when they are available.

What this page is

The manuscript's examples, three of its results, and a place to try Jeus.

Every number on this page is copied from the manuscript's released records: one evaluation per agent, model and question, with confidence intervals, the proprietary-model comparisons and the clinical results in the manuscript itself. The page does not describe how the agent is built.

To try Jeus yourself, sign in with a Google account at the live demo. Ask one question and two agents answer it side by side on GPT-6 Sol and the same files: Jeus, and OpenAI's own agent, Codex CLI. Token usage and time show under every reply, and each agent's total tokens and cost show at the end. You get a private workspace with example data, you can upload files of your own, and each person may use $5 of model usage a day.

Codex CLI, Claude Code and Biomni A1 ran with the same models, the same data and the same limits as Jeus. Results depend on the model behind each agent as much as on the agent; the examples above show both.
